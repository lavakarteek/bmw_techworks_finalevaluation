BMW Vehicle Alert
==================

BMW Vehicle Alert detects dangerous vehicle telemetry and publishes alerts for
critical events. It validates incoming SQS messages, evaluates temperature and
fault-code rules, sends critical alerts through Amazon SNS, and stores an audit
record in Amazon S3.
